# Creative Concept V2 — Real Street / Cartoon Interior / Behind the Scenes

**Documented:** 2026-10-21
**Status:** The overall real → animation → real structure is user-approved. The shop category, visual gags and pacing below remain PROPOSALS pending user selection.

## User's updated concept (supersedes dark-city/superhero treatment)
- Confident, composed, stylish/professional character walks naturally along an ordinary real-looking street.
- He pauses and turns to look at a storefront; the sign, windows and facade are visible in a realistic shot.
- The camera magically crosses the storefront/door into the shop. From here onward the environment is stylized 3D cartoon.
- The camera explores fantastical, funny, character-driven activity and playful level-up effects inside the shop.
- The camera curves around the main action to reveal a stylized, professional film-production crew working behind the scene.
- A second magical transition returns to the real-looking street, close-up on the same character.
- He gives a restrained wink and slight smile, turns toward the street and calmly continues walking.
- **Explicit exclusions:** no dark, cold, gloomy city; no old light-spreading-to-city rescue plot; not a Batman-inspired hero.

## Creative priority
The cartoon interior and playful gags are the primary creative payoff, not merely a pretty architectural makeover. The reveal of a full production team should feel like a surprising SECOND payoff. Create a mini-comedy with setup, escalation and payoff.

## Leading candidate: Fast-food shop — 'The ingredients are shooting a commercial'
**Logline:** When the camera enters a seemingly ordinary fast-food shop, ingredients behave like fussy actors on a film set; amid spectacular kitchen choreography they assemble an irresistible hero burger. Camera swings past the beauty shot to expose the stylized professional film crew responsible for the magic.

### Proposed gags (not final)
1. On a spotless cartoon griddle, two cheeky sausages try to perform synchronized acrobatic dance as the chef struggles to herd them with spatulas. One takes an exaggerated star pose for the camera.
2. Nearby, a tiny tomato and a stubborn pickle compete for their place in the hero burger; a lettuce leaf unfolds dramatically like a theatrical curtain / green carpet, sending one sliding toward the burger stage.
3. Chef finally brings the unruly performers together. Cheese, lettuce, buns and fillings arrange themselves into a visually beautiful towering burger in time with a musical beat. Hero-food shot.
4. Camera continues its move BEHIND the dazzling image: studio lights, gimbal/camera, monitor, boom, and a creative director plus team are orchestrating the cartoon production. A visual clapboard/shutter gag can motivate the reveal and next transition.
5. Return to the identical realistic character outside. Subtle knowing wink and smile; he walks on.

**Important art direction:** Do NOT anthropomorphize all ingredients; pick 2–3 memorable characters for readability, avoiding busy frames and off-putting 'living food being cooked/eaten'. Keep surfaces, dishes and final food appetizing.

### Approximate timing, 24–27 seconds
- 0–5 s: realistic confident walk, pause, look, shop exterior.
- 5–7 s: magic lens/door transition.
- 7–12 s: sausage/chef visual comedy.
- 12–16 s: tomato-pickle-lettuce gag (comic escalation).
- 16–19 s: synchronized hero-food payoff.
- 19–22 s: camera curves to production-crew reveal.
- 22–26 s: reverse transition, real character micro-smile/wink and continues walking.
- Brand can be placed as controlled post-production overlay or sonic logo; do not rely on generated in-scene text.

## Alternative store categories (also not final)
- **Bakery / patisserie**: mischievous dough escapes, macarons march in formation, chocolate wave becomes polished product shot. Highest fairy-tale charm.
- **Fashion boutique**: mannequins become runway performers, clothing changes on musical beats, behind-the-scenes styled photo shoot. More elegant and less slapstick.
